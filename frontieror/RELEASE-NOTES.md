# Research archive: release notes

Saved solutions, solver code and experimental records from the FrontierOR investigations, with the checks used to evaluate the results.

## Explore the evidence

The archive covers the factory-scheduling study, the earlier public-large campaign and the 180-task public-small campaign. Each study keeps its own results, code and validation records.

[Browse the studies](README.md) · [Methods and study limitations](METHODS.md) · [Verification report](verification-public.json)

## Figures and recordings

The article includes the original Sagent screenshot and Trackinizer recording. Constructed illustrations and sequences reconstructed from saved solutions are identified in their captions.

## Additional source materials

The evaluator and historical comparator support files are available from their upstream projects. The archive links to these sources; the detailed [file-inclusion record](RELEASE-RECORDS.md#withheld-materials) explains which materials are distributed here.
