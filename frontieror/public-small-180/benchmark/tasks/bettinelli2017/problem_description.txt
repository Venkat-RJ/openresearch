# Problem Description

A collector wishes to fill a single knapsack with a subset of available items so as to maximize the total profit of the selected items. There are n items, each characterized by a positive integer profit and a positive integer weight, and the knapsack has a positive integer capacity. The total weight of all items exceeds the knapsack capacity, but every individual item's weight is at most the capacity, so each item could fit on its own. In addition to the knapsack, an undirected conflict graph is provided: the graph has one vertex per item, and each edge connects a pair of items that are incompatible with one another, meaning they cannot both be placed in the knapsack together. The input therefore specifies the number of items, the profit and weight of each item, the integer knapsack capacity, and the full list of edges in the conflict graph (given as pairs of item indices).

The task is to choose which items to place in the knapsack. The chosen set of items must satisfy two requirements simultaneously: the sum of the weights of the chosen items must not exceed the knapsack capacity, and for every pair of items connected by an edge in the conflict graph, at most one of the two may be chosen. The goal is to find a selection of items satisfying both requirements whose total profit is as large as possible.
