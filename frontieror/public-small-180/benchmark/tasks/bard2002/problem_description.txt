# Problem Description

A fleet of identical vehicles, all based at a single depot, must pick up goods from a set of customers. Each customer has a known positive demand, a known service time, and a time window defined by an earliest ready time and a latest due date. The depot has coordinates, a scheduling horizon, and zero demand and service time. Every pair of locations (including the depot) has a known travel time, which equals the Euclidean distance between them truncated to one decimal digit; vehicle speed is one, so travel time and distance are interchangeable. Service time at each customer is folded into the outbound travel times from that customer: the effective travel time from any customer to any other location equals the base travel time plus the service time at the origin customer. Furthermore, if the difference between the destination's ready time and the origin customer's due date exceeds this adjusted travel time, the effective travel time is replaced by that difference. The resulting travel times satisfy the triangle inequality.

The goal is to determine a set of routes, each starting and ending at the depot, that collectively visit every customer exactly once, while using the fewest vehicles possible. Each route is a sequence of customers served by a single vehicle. The accumulated load picked up along any route must never exceed the vehicle capacity. A vehicle departing from a customer must do so no earlier than that customer's ready time and no later than that customer's due date. When a vehicle travels directly from one customer to another, its departure time at the second customer must be at least as large as its departure time at the first customer plus the effective travel time between them. The accumulated load aboard a vehicle when it departs a customer must be at least as large as that customer's demand and must also be at least as large as the accumulated load at the preceding customer on the route plus the current customer's demand. Arcs between pairs of customers that are infeasible due to time window or capacity incompatibility are excluded in advance.

The primary quantity to be minimized is the number of vehicles dispatched from the depot, which equals the number of routes used.
