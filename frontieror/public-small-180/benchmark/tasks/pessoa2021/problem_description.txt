# Problem Description

A delivery company operates a fleet of identical vehicles out of a single depot to serve a set of customers. The road network is modeled as a complete directed graph on the depot and the customers, with a known transportation cost on every arc between each pair of locations. The input data specifies the number of customers, the number of available vehicles, the common capacity of each vehicle, the two-dimensional coordinates of the depot and every customer, and the matrix of pairwise transportation costs.

Customer demands are not known exactly at the time routes must be planned. For each customer the company knows a nominal demand and a maximum demand deviation (the largest additional amount by which the actual demand could exceed the nominal value). To express the limited amount of total deviation the company is willing to hedge against, an uncertainty budget Gamma is given. In any realized demand scenario, each customer's actual demand lies between its nominal value and its nominal value plus its deviation, and the sum over all customers of the fractional deviation (the realized increase above nominal divided by that customer's maximum deviation) may not exceed Gamma. In other words, at most Gamma customers can simultaneously deviate fully from their nominal demands, with partial deviations counted proportionally.

The company must design a set of vehicle routes, one per vehicle, such that every route starts and ends at the depot and visits a sequence of distinct customers. Every customer must appear on exactly one route, and the total number of routes used must equal the number of available vehicles. The essential operational requirement is robustness: the routes must be feasible under every demand realization allowed by the uncertainty budget. That is, for every scenario in which customer demands respect the nominal-plus-deviation bounds and the total fractional deviation does not exceed Gamma, the total demand of the customers visited by each route must not exceed the vehicle capacity.

The objective is to minimize the total transportation cost, defined as the sum of the arc costs along the chosen routes.
