# Problem Description

A logistics company operates a set of candidate facility locations and must serve a set of customers. The number of customers is twice the number of facilities. Each facility has a capacity limit, and each customer has a known demand. The company must decide which facilities to open and assign every customer to exactly one open facility, so that the total demand assigned to any facility does not exceed that facility's capacity. A customer can only be served by a facility that is open.

Two competing cost structures are evaluated simultaneously. Under the first cost structure, opening a given facility incurs a fixed cost specific to that facility, and assigning a given customer to that facility incurs an assignment cost specific to that facility-customer pair. The first total cost is the sum of all fixed opening costs plus all assignment costs under this first structure. The second cost structure is defined identically in form but with an entirely independent set of fixed costs and assignment costs, yielding a second total cost. Both total costs are to be minimized simultaneously; among non-dominated solutions, improving one cost necessarily comes at the expense of the other.

The input data specifies the number of facilities and the number of customers, a demand for each customer, a capacity for each facility, fixed opening costs for each facility under each of the two cost structures, and assignment costs for each facility-customer pair under each of the two cost structures. All costs are integers, and all demands and capacities are integers.

A solution is called non-dominated if no other solution satisfying all the requirements achieves a value at least as good on both cost structures and strictly better on at least one. The goal is to identify the complete set of non-dominated cost pairs. In particular, using a weighted combination with equal weights of one-half on each cost structure, the reported single combined cost is one-half times the first total cost plus one-half times the second total cost, evaluated at the non-dominated solution that minimizes this weighted sum.

Each customer must be assigned to exactly one facility. The total demand of all customers assigned to a given facility must not exceed that facility's capacity multiplied by whether the facility is open; if the facility is closed, no customer may be assigned to it. In addition, each individual customer assignment is linked to the opening status of the corresponding facility: a customer may be assigned to a facility only if that facility is open. All opening and assignment decisions are indivisible, meaning each facility is either fully open or fully closed, and each customer is either fully assigned to a given facility or not at all.
