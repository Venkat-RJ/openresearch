# Problem Description

A collection of items is available, each with a known profit and a known weight, both of which are positive integers. A single knapsack is available with a given capacity that is also a positive integer. The total weight of all items exceeds the knapsack capacity, so not every item can be packed. Each individual item's weight does not exceed the capacity, meaning every item could in principle fit on its own. For each item, the decision is whether to pack it into the knapsack or leave it out; partial inclusion of an item is not allowed. The total weight of all packed items must not exceed the knapsack capacity. The goal is to select a subset of items to pack so as to maximize the total profit of the packed items.
